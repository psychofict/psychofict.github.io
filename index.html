Research highlights and experimental scale

MSc research: CW-BASS and FARCLUSS

DeepLabV3+ with ResNet-50/101 on Pascal VOC 2012 and Cityscapes; evaluated 1/16–1/2 labeled-data splits. CW-BASS combines confidence-weighted pseudo-label loss, adaptive thresholding and Sobel boundary supervision, reaching 65.9 mIoU on Cityscapes with 100 labeled images (1/30). FARCLUSS combines top-K fuzzy labels, entropy-based uncertainty weighting, adaptive class rebalancing and a 128-D prototype-contrastive head; ResNet-101 at 1/8 labeled data: 78.2 mIoU on VOC and 78.5 on Cityscapes. Training recipes: 80 epochs (VOC), 240 (Cityscapes), batch 4/2; CUDA multi-GPU DataParallel support in CW-BASS.

Independent foundation-model research: CW-BASS v2 and PixCon

DINOv2-Base (ViT-B/14) with DPT-lite on Pascal VOC, Cityscapes and ADE20K. CW-BASS v2: 518×518 crops, 60 epochs, batch 4, AdamW, BF16 AMP, 5% held-out calibration and a reliability gate evaluated across six DINOv2 teacher settings. Pascal VOC 1/8: 86.19 ± 1.82 mIoU (3 seeds); Cityscapes 83.96 and ADE20K 50.58 (+1.5 over the strict-threshold reproduction). PixCon: 60/120-epoch recipes, batch 4 (effective 16 with gradient accumulation), clean-positive pixel-contrastive branch with 256 entries/class and τ=0.1. Pascal VOC 1/8: 87.90 mIoU (3-seed mean, versus 87.01 for the matched reproduction); Cityscapes 83.88 and ADE20K 49.23 (single-seed results). The contrastive head is training-only and adds no inference-time parameters.
